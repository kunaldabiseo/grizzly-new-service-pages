Straight answer first

What a co-working space insulation contractor does

A co-working space insulation contractor puts sound and thermal insulation in the walls and ceilings of your space and seals the gaps sound travels through. Glass, doors, booths, ceilings and HVAC come from other trades. Here is how it splits.

  • Sound insulation in office, meeting and call room wallsGrizzly
  • Closing the path over the top of walls (plenum barriers)Grizzly
  • Sealing outlets, wall tops, bottoms and penetrationsGrizzly
  • Exterior wall and top-floor insulation for comfortGrizzly
  • Glass fronts, doors and door sealsGlazing and door suppliers
  • Prefab phone booths and podsBooth manufacturer
  • Ceiling tiles, acoustic panels and sound maskingInteriors or acoustics vendor
  • Room acoustics for podcast and recordingAcoustical consultant
  • Heating and cooling for small officesHVAC contractor

The short answer

How to insulate a co-working space

Decide how private each room needs to be before the walls go up. Fill every wall between offices, meeting rooms and call rooms with sound insulation, close the path over the top for rooms that need real privacy, and seal outlets and gaps. Remember that glass fronts and doors set the limit, so match the wall to the weakest part. Plan the work around member hours and your landlord's rules.

Glass-front offices

Glass-front offices: being honest about the weak link

Glass fronts sell offices. They also let more sound through than an insulated wall. Insulation makes the solid walls better, but the glass and door decide how private the room can get.

Privacy is only as strong as the weakest part

Insulated wallsGlass frontDoor

What we improve

  • Solid side and back walls filled with sound insulation
  • The path over the top of the room closed
  • Gaps at the glass frame where it meets solid walls sealed
  • Outlets and penetrations sealed

What the glass supplier decides

  • Single or double glazing, and laminated glass
  • Door type, seals and bottom sweeps
  • Frame and mullion details

Questions

Co-working space insulation contractor FAQs

Questions operators ask usTap a question to see the answer

What does a co-working space insulation contractor do?

A co-working space insulation contractor like Grizzly installs sound and thermal insulation in the walls and ceilings of private offices, meeting rooms and call rooms, closes the path over the top of walls and seals the gaps sound travels through. Glass fronts, doors, phone booths, ceiling tiles and HVAC come from other trades.

Why can members hear each other through the walls?

Usually a mix of empty wall cavities, walls that stop at the ceiling grid so sound goes over the top, back-to-back outlets, gaps at the floor, door gaps, glass fronts and shared ducts. We find and fix the ones that are ours and point out the rest.

Will insulation make our private offices soundproof?

No room is truly soundproof. Filling the walls, closing the path over the top and sealing gaps make normal conversation hard to make out, which is the right target for most private offices and meeting rooms. Glass fronts and doors set the limit.

What is STC?

Sound Transmission Class, defined in ASTM E413, is a single-number rating of how well a wall blocks airborne sound in a lab. Higher is better. Real rooms usually perform below the lab number because of doors, glass and gaps.

Do glass-front offices work for private calls?

They can work for everyday calls if the solid walls are insulated, the top is closed and gaps are sealed. The glass and door decide how private the room can get. For truly confidential rooms, a solid front may be the better choice.

Can you build a podcast or recording room?

We install the insulation and sealing for a podcast room, including decoupled wall layers, to the design an acoustical consultant specifies. Recording rooms need sound kept out as well as in, so they need more than a normal office wall.

Can you work while our space is open?

Yes. We do noisy work outside your busiest member hours, close off one area at a time, cover floors, furniture and IT, and clean up before members return.

What does our landlord need from you?

Usually product data sheets for every material, insurance certificates naming the landlord, and agreement to the building rules for elevators, loading and noisy work. We provide those up front.

How much does co-working space insulation cost?

Co-working space insulation cost depends on the number and type of rooms, the privacy level for each, whether walls are open or finished, ceiling access, working hours and building rules. We quote from a free walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
