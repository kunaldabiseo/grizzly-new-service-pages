Straight answer first

What a surgical center insulation contractor does

A surgical center insulation contractor insulates and seals the walls, ceilings and roof of an ambulatory surgery center, so rooms are tighter, quieter and more comfortable. It works to the wall designs, life safety plan and infection controls set by others, and it does not touch medical gas, OR ceilings or the air systems that keep the rooms clean.

The short answer

How to insulate a surgical center

Get the shell right first: insulate the roof and exterior walls so the air system is not fighting the weather. Seal and insulate OR and procedure room walls tightly, using products the design team approves above the ceiling. Contain sterile processing and mechanical noise, give pre-op, PACU and consult rooms sound insulation where there are walls, and do it all on non-case days inside your infection controls.

What does a surgical center insulation contractor do?

A surgical center insulation contractor like Grizzly insulates and seals the walls, ceilings and roof of an ambulatory surgery center so rooms are tighter, quieter and more comfortable. It works to the rated wall designs, life safety plan and infection controls set by others, and leaves air systems, OR ceilings and medical gas to specialist trades.

Can you work in or near our operating rooms?

Yes, when the room is shut down for the job and inside the infection controls your team sets. We use the products the design team approves above the ceiling and hand the room back for cleaning, inspection and air balancing.

What are unrestricted, semi-restricted and restricted areas?

They are the zones of a surgery suite. Unrestricted areas are public and patient spaces, semi-restricted areas are support spaces and corridors around the ORs, and restricted areas are the operating rooms themselves. The rules for anyone working there get tighter as you move in.

Does insulation help an OR hold pressure?

Sealing gaps makes the room leak less air, which helps the air system do its job. The pressure itself, air changes and filtration are designed and balanced by your mechanical engineer.

What Life Safety Code rules apply to surgery centers?

Medicare-certified ASCs must meet the Life Safety Code provisions for ambulatory health care occupancies, regardless of the number of patients served. Insulation in rated walls has to match each wall's listed design, and penetrations are firestopped by a firestop installer.

Can you quiet our sterile processing room?

We insulate and seal the walls between the decontamination side, the clean side, the ORs and staff rooms. Pressure, humidity and the sterilizers themselves belong to your HVAC and equipment vendors.

We are converting an office building into an ASC. Where do we start?

Start with the shell: roof and exterior walls insulated and air sealed so the new air system can hold temperature and humidity. Then rated walls to their listed designs, OR and procedure room walls sealed and insulated, and support rooms, all inspected before drywall.

When can you work without affecting cases?

On weekends, nights or non-case days. Restricted rooms are only worked on when shut down for the job, and every area is cleaned and inspected before cases resume.

How much does surgical center insulation cost?

Surgical center insulation cost depends on how much work sits in semi-restricted or restricted areas, rated wall requirements, whether walls are open or finished, the infection controls required, your schedule and whether the roof and exterior are included.
