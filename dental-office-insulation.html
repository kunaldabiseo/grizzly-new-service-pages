Straight answer first

What a dental office insulation contractor does

A dental office insulation contractor fills and seals the walls, ceilings and roof of a practice so handpiece and equipment noise stays where it starts, treatment conversations stay private and the office stays comfortable. It does not install X-ray shielding, gas or vacuum piping, or the equipment itself. Here is the split.

The short answer

How to insulate a dental office

Contain the loudest things first: insulate the equipment room's walls and ceiling and seal its penetrations, while keeping the ventilation the equipment needs. Then put solid, insulated walls between the clinical area and the waiting room, and give consult rooms full privacy. Insulate whatever operatory walls the layout has, close the path over the top, and finish with the roof and outside walls.

Questions

Dental office insulation contractor FAQs

What does a dental office insulation contractor do?

A dental office insulation contractor like Grizzly fills and seals the walls, ceilings and roof of a practice so equipment and handpiece noise stays contained, consult conversations stay private and the office stays comfortable. X-ray shielding, gas and vacuum piping and the equipment itself come from dental specialists and other trades.

How do we quiet our compressor and vacuum pump?

Insulate the equipment room's walls and ceiling, seal its penetrations and the door, and keep the openings it needs for air. Ventilation is sized by your HVAC contractor and isolation pads come from your equipment installer.

Will insulating the equipment room make it overheat?

Not if it is done right. Compressors and vacuum pumps need fresh air and ventilation. We insulate around the vents, intakes and exhaust your HVAC contractor and equipment installer specify and never cover them.

Can insulation make open operatories private?

No. In an open bay there are no walls between chairs to fill, so patients will hear each other. Insulation helps the walls around the bay, the ceiling above it and the wall to the waiting room. Closed operatories are where insulation does the most.

Can patients in the waiting room hear the drill?

Often, if the wall between the clinic and the waiting room is empty or stops at the ceiling. Filling it, closing the path above and sealing gaps makes the biggest difference. Music or sound masking helps cover what is left.

Do you install X-ray shielding?

No. X-ray, pano and CBCT shielding is decided by a qualified expert under your state's radiation rules and installed by a shielding contractor. We insulate the walls around those rooms.

When is the best time to insulate a dental office?

During a build-out, before drywall, while walls and ceilings are open. In an existing office we work on closed days, evenings or weekends, one area at a time.

Do you work around our patient schedule?

Yes. Noisy and dusty work happens on your closed days or after the last patient, chairs and equipment are covered, and everything is cleaned before patients come back.

How much does dental office insulation cost?

Dental office insulation cost depends on the equipment room, how many operatory, consult and waiting room walls need work, whether they are open or finished, ceiling access, your schedule and whether the roof is included. We quote from a free walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
