Straight answer first

What a shopping center insulation contractor does

A shopping center insulation contractor insulates and seals the roofs, exterior walls and demising walls across a center, so vacant units are ready to lease, tenants have fewer reasons to complain and the property uses less energy. It works for the owner or the tenant, building by building and unit by unit. Roofing, storefronts and HVAC belong to other trades. Here is the split.

Unit 101Grizzly

  • Under-deck roof insulation, building by building
  • Demising walls between units, to the deck
  • Exterior walls, end caps and rear walls
  • White-box insulation in vacant units
  • Common area walls, corridors and restrooms

Unit 102Roofing and envelope trades

  • Roof membrane and above-deck insulation with a reroof
  • Storefront glass and entrances
  • Rear doors and service doors
  • Parapets, flashing and drains

Unit 103Other trades

  • Rooftop units, controls and metering
  • Restaurant hoods and grease exhaust
  • Tenant fixtures and finishes
  • Lighting and parking lot work

The short answer

How to insulate a shopping center

Treat the center as a portfolio: survey every building's roof and walls, then plan insulation around the roof replacement schedule. Insulate vacant units while they are empty, and take demising walls to the deck as units turn over. Fix end caps and rear walls that cost tenants comfort, and track the results through your energy benchmarking.

Benchmarking

Energy benchmarking for larger centers

Colorado's Building Performance program requires owners of commercial buildings of 50,000 square feet or larger to benchmark whole-building energy use every year and meet set performance targets. Insulation is one of the envelope measures that shows up in that number.

Questions

Shopping center insulation contractor FAQs

InformationAsk here

What does a shopping center insulation contractor do?

A shopping center insulation contractor like Grizzly insulates and seals the roofs, exterior walls and demising walls across a center, working for the owner or for tenants, building by building and unit by unit. Roofing, storefronts, HVAC and tenant fixtures come from other trades.

Should we insulate when we replace a roof?

Yes, it is usually the cheapest moment to add insulation above the deck, and that goes in your roofer's bid. Buildings that are not due for a reroof can be insulated from below in the meantime.

When is the best time to insulate a unit?

When it is vacant. In a dark shell or before a white box closes up, the roof, walls and demising walls are all reachable without working around a tenant.

How do we stop tenants complaining about each other?

Fill the demising wall between them, take it to the deck and seal outlets, pipes and the floor line. Restaurant smells also need separate exhaust and pressure balance, and gyms need flooring and isolation for impact.

Does our center have to benchmark its energy use?

Colorado's Building Performance program requires owners of commercial buildings of 50,000 square feet or larger to benchmark whole-building energy use every year and meet performance targets. Confirm which of your buildings are covered with the state program.

Who pays for insulation in a shopping center?

It depends on the leases. Often the owner pays for roofs, shells and vacant units, common area work is shared through common area maintenance, and tenants pay for their own interiors. We can price work so it splits cleanly.

Can you work without closing the center?

Yes. Occupied units are done overnight or before opening, vacant units during the day, and roof and common area work is staged away from shoppers with parking kept open.

Can you price a whole center in phases?

Yes. We walk every building and price building by building and unit by unit, so the work can be funded through your capital plan over time.

How much does shopping center insulation cost?

Shopping center insulation cost depends on roof area per building, vacancies, demising wall length and access, end caps and rear walls, working hours and how the work is phased. We quote from a free walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
