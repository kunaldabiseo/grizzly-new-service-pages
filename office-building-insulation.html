The short answer

How to insulate an office building

Start where the complaints are. Insulate the roof or top-floor ceiling, then exterior walls and the gaps around windows, and seal the air leaks that make perimeter offices drafty. For privacy, fill office and conference room walls with sound insulation and close the path over the top of the wall. In an occupied building, do the work in stages after hours, and use spray foam only where the space can be emptied.

Roof and top floor

Fixing the top floor: above the ceiling or at the roof

Top-floor tenants feel the roof first. In many older office buildings the insulation is thin, crushed or missing above the ceiling tiles. There are two places to fix it, and a roof replacement is the best moment to choose.

Illustrative Section through a top floor, not to scale.

Option

Above the ceiling tiles

  • Fastest to add in an occupied building
  • Has to be redone around every tile or fixture someone lifts
  • The plenum above stays at outside-ish temperatures
Option

At the roof deck

  • Keeps the whole plenum inside the insulated envelope
  • Protects ducts and pipes up there too
  • Needs lift access and more planning
Best moment

When the roof is replaced

  • Insulation above the deck goes on with the new roof, by your roofer
  • We handle anything inside: removal, walls, sealing
  • Ask us to walk it with your roofer before the bid

Speech privacy

Why your conference room is not private

Most office walls stop at the ceiling grid. Sound goes straight up, across the open space above the tiles and down into the next room. Filling the wall helps, but closing that path over the top is usually what makes the difference. Switch between the three walls.

Empty wall to the grid

Sound passes through the hollow wall and floods over the top through the open plenum. Conversations carry clearly into the next room.

Privacy

General guide, not a sound test. Doors, ducts and back-to-back outlets leak sound too, and we seal what is ours to seal.

Perimeter offices

Cold corner offices and drafty window walls

The best offices often have the worst comfort. Corner and perimeter rooms have the most exterior wall and glass, and the gaps around windows and behind spandrel panels let outside air in. Insulation and sealing fix the walls; the glass is a glazing job.

Insulation for tenant improvements and suite build-outs

Spray foam and tenants

Spray foam in an occupied building: when it fits

EPA guidance is direct: during spray foam installation, other trade workers and building occupants should vacate the premises, and re-entry timing comes from the foam manufacturer. In an office building, that decides where foam makes sense.

Can the space be emptied?

YesFoam fits

Space can be emptied

  • Vacant suites and shell space
  • Roof decks over empty floors
  • Weekend work with the area closed and ventilated
  • Re-entry timed to the manufacturer's guidance
NoUse something else

People stay in the space

  • Mineral wool or fiberglass batts in walls
  • Rigid board where a thin, high-value layer is needed
  • Sealants and gaskets for air leaks

Building performance standards and your office building

Colorado's Building Performance program covers commercial, multifamily and public buildings of 50,000 square feet or larger. Owners benchmark whole-building energy use every year and work toward energy and emissions targets. Check your building's size.

Questions

Office building insulation contractor FAQs

What does an office building insulation contractor do?

An office building insulation contractor like Grizzly insulates and seals roofs, top-floor ceilings, exterior walls and the gaps around windows, and adds sound insulation to office, conference room and demising walls. HVAC balancing, windows, ceiling tiles, fire-rated wall design and roofing belong to other trades.

Why is our top floor always too hot?

The top floor sits right under the roof, and many older office buildings have thin, crushed or missing insulation above the ceiling. Insulating at the roof deck or above the ceiling, and sealing the edges, usually makes the biggest difference. A roof replacement is the best moment to add insulation above the deck.

How do we make a conference room more private?

Fill the walls with sound insulation and close the path over the top. Most office walls stop at the ceiling grid, so sound travels through the open space above the tiles. A sealed, insulated barrier above the wall or a wall taken up to the deck fixes that path. Doors and ducts still need attention.

Can you work while tenants are in the building?

Yes. We work after hours or on weekends, area by area, cover furniture, floors and IT, and leave spaces clean for the morning. With people in the space we use batts, mineral wool and board, not spray foam.

Can you use spray foam in an occupied office building?

Only where the space can be emptied. EPA guidance says building occupants should vacate during spray foam installation, with re-entry timed to the manufacturer's guidance. That makes foam a good fit for vacant suites and closed-off areas, and other materials better where people stay.

Do tenant improvements have to meet the energy code?

When a tenant improvement opens up exterior walls or the roof, the energy code your jurisdiction has adopted can require insulation on the parts you alter. Your architect or building department confirms exactly what applies to your project.

Does insulation help with Building Performance Colorado?

It can be one part of the plan. The program covers commercial buildings of 50,000 square feet or larger with annual benchmarking and energy and emissions targets. HVAC, lighting and controls usually carry more of the savings; we give your energy consultant real numbers for the envelope.

Why are our corner offices so cold?

Corner and perimeter offices have the most exterior wall and glass. Thin wall insulation and gaps around window frames and spandrel panels let cold air in. We insulate and seal the walls and window perimeters; failed glass is a glazing contractor's job.

How much does office building insulation cost?

Office building insulation cost depends on where the work is (roof deck, above the ceiling or walls), access around ceilings and ducts, whether tenants are in place, material choice, removal of old insulation and protection and cleanup. We quote from a free walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
