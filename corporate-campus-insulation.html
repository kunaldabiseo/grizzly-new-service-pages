What a corporate campus insulation contractor does

A corporate campus insulation contractor surveys, insulates and seals the building envelopes across a campus, and keeps the work consistent from one building to the next. The mechanical, glazing, roofing and specialty systems on a campus belong to their own trades.

The short answer

How to insulate a corporate campus

Treat it as a program, not a pile of separate jobs. Survey every building's envelope once, rank the work by comfort complaints, roof timing and building age, and start with one building to prove the approach. Then roll the same details and materials across the campus, building by building, and document each one so energy and capital teams can track the results.

Parking structures

Cold floors over the parking garage

When offices, a lobby or a cafeteria sit over a parking level, the floor is open to outside air from below. People feel it as cold floors and drafts. Insulating the garage ceiling fixes it, as long as the material suits an open, sometimes wet, space.

Specialty spaces

Locker rooms, labs, cafeterias and auditoriums

The spaces that make a campus a campus are also the ones that are hardest on the building envelope. Pick one.

Locker rooms and showers

Moisture from showers and steam rooms gets into walls and ceilings, and it is common to find wet, moldy insulation behind tile.

Ours

Wet insulation out, closed-cell foam or a sealed assembly in, and a continuous vapor seal on the warm, wet side.

Theirs

Exhaust fans and dehumidification from your mechanical contractor.

Connectors

Skybridges, links and glass connectors

The walkways that tie a campus together are usually mostly glass, exposed on every side, and joined to two buildings that move independently. They are often the coldest walk of the day.

Constant change

Moves, remodels and the insulation nobody put back

Campuses change all the time. Every reconfiguration, cabling run, roof repair and HVAC swap opens ceilings and walls, and the insulation that comes out rarely goes back the way it was. Over a few years, a well insulated building can quietly end up full of gaps.

    Questions

    Corporate campus insulation contractor FAQs

    0 of 9 read
    What does a corporate campus insulation contractor do?

    A corporate campus insulation contractor like Grizzly surveys, insulates and seals the building envelopes across a campus, keeps details and materials consistent from building to building, and documents the work for facilities, capital and energy teams. Mechanical, glazing, roofing and specialty systems belong to their own trades.

    How do we decide which building to insulate first?

    Rank buildings by roof work coming due, comfort complaints and age. Insulation above the roof deck is cheapest when a roof is replaced, so buildings with roof work due soon often go first. We survey every building and give you a ranked list for capital planning.

    Can you work across several buildings at once?

    Yes. We survey the whole campus, agree details and materials on a pilot building, then roll the same standards across the rest in the order your budget allows, with one point of contact for every building.

    How do you insulate a parking garage ceiling?

    With materials suited to an open, sometimes wet garage, such as mineral wool board, faced boards or spray-applied insulation with an approved covering, chosen with your fire marshal. Sprinklers, lights and drains stay clear, and edges and penetrations are sealed.

    Can you work in labs and locker rooms?

    We insulate and seal the envelope around labs and wet rooms. Lab exhaust, fume hoods and pressure control belong to your lab planner and mechanical contractor, and locker room exhaust to your HVAC contractor. In locker rooms we remove wet insulation and install a sealed, moisture-resistant assembly.

    Can you meet our vendor requirements?

    Tell us what your vendor program asks for. We provide certificates of insurance, vendor setup paperwork, safety data sheets and signed site rules, and our crews follow your badging, parking and permit rules.

    Do you provide documentation for energy reporting?

    Yes. Each building gets a record of the areas insulated, materials, thickness, rated R-values, air sealing and before and after photos. Energy savings modeling and reporting belong to your energy consultant.

    Why are the floors cold above our parking garage?

    The underside of that floor is open to outside air on the parking level. Insulating the garage ceiling and sealing the slab edges keeps the occupied floor above warmer.

    How much does corporate campus insulation cost?

    Corporate campus insulation cost depends on the number of buildings and how they are phased, the building types involved, access and work hours, vendor requirements, removal of old insulation and the documentation your team needs. We quote from a free campus walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
