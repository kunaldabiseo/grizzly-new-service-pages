Straight answer first

What a hotel insulation contractor does

A hotel insulation contractor insulates and seals the walls between guest rooms, the corridor walls, the floors and ceilings between stories, the roof and the outside walls, plus back of house rooms like the pool, laundry and kitchen. The goal is quieter rooms, even temperatures from the top floor to the lobby and less load on heating and cooling. Doors, windows, room HVAC units and finishes come from other trades.

The short answer

How to insulate a hotel

Start with the guest rooms that get complaints. Fill the walls between rooms with sound insulation and seal every outlet, pipe and gap, then treat the corridor wall and the ceiling cavity. Insulate the roof and the outside walls of top floor and corner rooms. Handle the pool and laundry on their own, because humid rooms need moisture control as well as insulation. Work in small groups of rooms so the hotel keeps selling the rest.

Where the noise gets in

Five ways sound gets into a guest room

A guest room has neighbors on both sides, a corridor in front and rooms above and below. Sound finds the weakest path, so one open outlet box can undo a good wall. Tap each path on the room plan.

The International Building Code sets these minimums for hotel rooms in new work (section 1206 in the 2021 edition, 1207 in 2024). Insulation is one part of a rated wall or floor; studs, drywall layers and sealing make up the rest. Your building official decides which edition applies.

Behind the scenes

Pool, laundry and the rest of the back of house

The rooms guests never see often cause the problems guests notice: a humid pool, a noisy laundry under a guest floor, a kitchen next to the ballroom. Pick a space.

Moisture first

Indoor pool

Warm, humid pool air pushes into the walls and roof. In cold weather it can condense inside them, which leads to rot, rust and mold.

Ours

Walls and roof insulated with the vapor control placed for a humid room, and gaps sealed so moist air stays out of the assemblies.

Other trades

Dehumidification and ventilation from your HVAC contractor, and pool finishes.

Questions

Hotel insulation contractor FAQs

Guest servicesQuestions at the front desk

What does a hotel insulation contractor do?

A hotel insulation contractor like Grizzly insulates and seals the walls between guest rooms, corridor walls, floors and ceilings between stories, the roof and outside walls, and back of house rooms like the pool and laundry. Doors, windows, room HVAC units and finishes come from other trades.

Can insulation stop guests hearing the next room?

Insulation filling the wall between rooms is a big part of it, but sound also leaks through outlet boxes, pipes, gaps at the floor and the corridor door. We fill the wall and seal those gaps. Doors and impact noise from above need work from other trades too.

What sound rating do hotel room walls need?

For new work, the International Building Code sets a minimum of STC 50 for walls and IIC 50 for floor-ceilings between sleeping units, or 45 when field tested. Insulation is one part of a rated assembly. Your building official decides what applies to your project.

Why are our top floor and corner rooms always cold?

Top floor rooms lose heat through the roof, and corner rooms have two outside walls and more glass. Topping up roof insulation and insulating and sealing the outside walls usually helps those rooms most.

Can you insulate while the hotel is open?

Yes. We take rooms out of order in small blocks, do noisy work during the day outside quiet hours, protect corridors and hand each room back clean to housekeeping.

What about our indoor pool?

Pool rooms need moisture control as well as insulation. We insulate the walls and roof with the vapor control placed for a humid room and seal the gaps. Dehumidification and ventilation come from your HVAC contractor.

When is the best time to add sound insulation?

During a renovation or conversion, while the walls between rooms are open. Adding it to finished rooms is possible but costs more per room.

Do you work on motels and older buildings?

Yes. Motels usually need attic insulation and walls between rooms taken to the roof deck. Older buildings converted to hotels need care with masonry walls, which we check before recommending anything.

How much does hotel insulation cost?

Hotel insulation cost depends on how many rooms need work, whether walls are open, the sound goal, roof area, back of house rooms and how many rooms can be out of order at once. We quote from a free walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
