Straight answer first

What a medical clinic insulation contractor does

A medical clinic insulation contractor fills and seals the walls, ceilings and roof of a clinic so conversations stay in the room, noise from neighbors stays out and patients stay comfortable. It does not replace doors, glass, HVAC or flooring, and it cannot stop every kind of noise. Here is the split.

The short answer

How to insulate a medical clinic

Seal the clinic off from its neighbors first: insulate the demising walls and make sure they reach the roof deck. Then give the private rooms real walls: exam, consult, counseling and telehealth rooms get sound insulation with the top closed and outlets sealed. Insulate the roof and exterior walls for comfort, and plan the work around the hours your clinic is open.

Two kinds of noise

Airborne noise vs impact noise

Voices, music and TVs travel through the air, and insulation in walls and ceilings is good at blocking them. Dropped weights, footsteps upstairs and treadmills shake the structure itself, and that needs a different fix.

What does a medical clinic insulation contractor do?

A medical clinic insulation contractor like Grizzly fills and seals the walls, ceilings and roof of a clinic so conversations stay in exam, consult and counseling rooms, noise from neighboring tenants stays out and patients stay comfortable. Doors, glass, HVAC, flooring and acoustic design come from other trades.

Can you stop noise from the gym next door?

Insulation in the demising wall, taken up to the roof deck and sealed, cuts music and voices a lot. Dropped weights and machine thuds travel through the structure, so they also need flooring and isolation on the gym's side.

Why can we hear the tenant next door over the wall?

In many retail centers the demising wall stops at the ceiling grid and the space above is shared. Sound goes straight over the top. Taking the wall to the deck or closing the path above, and filling the wall, fixes most of it.

How do you set up a private telehealth room?

Fill every wall with sound insulation, close the path over the top, seal outlets and gaps and fit a sealed door. To keep noise out of the microphone, keep the room away from the waiting room and front desk and add soft finishes inside. HHS expects telehealth to be conducted in private settings.

Will insulation make our counseling rooms private?

It makes a big difference when the walls are filled on every side, the top is closed and every gap is sealed. Doors, glass and shared ductwork set the final limit, so we point those out on the walk-through.

Can insulation fix our cold waiting room by the storefront?

Partly. Most of the heat moves through the storefront glass, which is a glazing fix. We insulate and seal the walls around and above the glass and the roof, which evens out the rest.

Do you work on clinics in retail centers?

Yes. Strip-center and retail clinics are common for us. We follow the landlord's rules and provide product data and insurance paperwork up front.

Can you work while our clinic is open?

Yes. We do noisy and dusty work on closed days or after hours, close off one area at a time and clean up before the next patients.

How much does medical clinic insulation cost?

Medical clinic insulation cost depends on the length and height of demising walls, how many private rooms need work, whether walls are open or finished, whether the roof is included, working hours and landlord rules. We quote from a free walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
