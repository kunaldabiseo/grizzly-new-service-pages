Straight answer first

What a motel insulation contractor does

A motel insulation contractor insulates and seals the attic over the rooms, the outside walls, the walls between rooms and the gaps where cold air and noise get in. In a motel that usually means topping up one long attic, sealing the ceilings below it, filling walls at the end rooms and treating the walls between rooms for sound. Roofing, doors, windows and the heating and cooling units come from other trades.

The short answer

How to insulate a motel

Start in the attic. Seal the ceiling below it, especially around lights, fans and hatches, then bring the insulation up to an even depth across every room. Next, fill the outside walls of the end rooms and the walls with plumbing, and add sound insulation in the walls between rooms when they are open. Finish by sealing gaps around wall units and pipes from inside each room.

Outside walls and end rooms

Insulating motel walls without gutting the rooms

End rooms and rooms with a bathroom on an outside wall are usually the coldest, and their pipes are the most likely to freeze. Many older motel walls are hollow or only partly filled. Depending on the wall, they can often be filled through small holes instead of tearing out drywall.

Questions

Motel insulation contractor FAQs

No.Guest register: questions

What does a motel insulation contractor do?

A motel insulation contractor like Grizzly insulates and seals a motel's attic, outside walls and walls between rooms, and seals the gaps where cold air and noise get in. Roofing, doors, windows and the heating and cooling units come from other trades.

Where should a motel start with insulation?

Usually the attic. One attic often runs over a whole row of rooms, so sealing the ceiling below it and bringing the insulation to an even depth helps every room at once.

Why are our end rooms always colder?

End rooms have an extra outside wall that takes the wind, and in older motels that wall is often hollow or only partly filled. Filling the wall and sealing around the wall unit usually helps most.

Can you insulate walls without tearing out drywall?

Often, yes. Many hollow walls can be filled through small holes from outside or inside, then patched. Some walls are not suited to it, which we check on the walk-through.

Can insulation stop guests hearing the next room?

Sound insulation in the wall between rooms helps a lot when the wall is open, along with sealing outlets and pipes. If the wall stops at the ceiling, the attic above it also needs attention. Doors and wall units are other trades.

Do you help with ice dams?

Ice dams on pitched motel roofs are often fed by warm air leaking into the attic. We seal the ceiling and add insulation. Roof ventilation, gutters and roofing are for your roofer.

Can you work while the motel is open?

Yes. Most attic work is done from the hatch without entering rooms, noisy work stays out of quiet hours, and rooms that need work are taken out in small groups and handed back clean.

We are converting the motel. Can you help?

Yes. For extended stay or apartments we insulate walls, floors and the attic as the design and code require, and seal every gap between units before the walls close.

How much does motel insulation cost?

Motel insulation cost depends on attic area and access, what insulation is already there, how much air sealing is needed, how many walls need filling, sound goals and how many rooms can be out at once. We quote from a free walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
