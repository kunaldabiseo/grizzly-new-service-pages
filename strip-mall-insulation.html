The short answer

How to insulate a strip mall

Close the open ceiling space between units first: fill the demising walls and take them up to the roof deck, sealing every pipe and duct that passes through. Then insulate the roof over each unit, the front wall behind the sign band and the rear block wall. Do vacant units completely while they are empty, and occupied units overnight.

Questions

Strip mall insulation contractor FAQs

Lift a tile to see the answer.

What does a strip mall insulation contractor do?

A strip mall insulation contractor like Grizzly insulates and seals the roof, the front and rear walls, the end walls and the demising walls between units in a strip mall. Roofing, storefront glass, signs, HVAC and tenant fixtures come from other trades.

Why is our end unit always colder than the rest?

An end unit has an extra exterior wall and more roof edge, and often a corner of glass, so it loses more heat than a middle unit. Insulating the end wall and sealing the roof to wall line usually helps most.

Can heat move from one unit to the next above the ceiling?

Yes, when the demising wall stops at the ceiling grid the space above is shared. Heat, air, smells and sound move between units. Taking the wall to the deck and sealing it closes that path.

Should we insulate the ceiling grid or the roof deck?

Insulation laid on a drop ceiling is easily disturbed by other trades and leaves the plenum outside the insulated space. Insulating at the deck keeps ducts and the plenum inside. We look at what you have and recommend one approach.

When is the best time to insulate a unit?

Between tenants. A vacant unit lets us reach the roof deck, the demising walls and the rear wall without working around a business.

Can you insulate while tenants are open?

Yes. Occupied units are done before opening or after closing, and the rear wall and roof are reached from outside or from vacant space where possible.

Do you insulate behind the sign band?

We insulate and seal the wall area above the storefront, including the parapet and the sign band cavity where we can reach it safely. The sign itself and its wiring are handled by your sign company and electrician.

Who pays for insulation in a strip mall?

It depends on the leases. Owners often pay for the roof, the shell and vacant units, and tenants pay for work inside their own space. We can price each unit separately so the split is clean.

How much does strip mall insulation cost?

Strip mall insulation cost depends on roof area, the number of units and end units, demising wall length, ceiling type, access at the rear and working hours. We quote from a free walk-through with an itemized scope letter.
